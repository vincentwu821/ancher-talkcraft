# Ancher TalkCraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy describes the Ancher TalkCraft skill and its distributed configuration. It does not replace the policies of Ancher, your host agent, or other services you choose to use.

## Information used

At your request, your host agent reads the references, ideas, notes, prompts, recorded footage, audio, and project files you provide or authorize it to access. These may include names, faces, voices, and other personal information. The skill instructs the agent to access only the notes and files needed for your project.

During production, the host saves scripts, transcripts, editing decisions, captions, rendered media, and a small project-state file in your project directory. That state may include Ancher note and conversation identifiers. These files are not intended to be written back into the distributable skill folder or published to this repository.

## Services and sharing

For reference analysis and script creation or revision, the host sends the selected references, your topic and instructions, and relevant project context to your authorized Ancher connection. Ancher stores submitted content and handles it under [Ancher’s Privacy Policy](https://ancher.ai/privacy). The skill does not bundle an MCP server or account credentials; you connect your own account.

For editing and publishing assets, the host may use local media tools or the transcription, image, search, music, and rendering services selected for your project. Depending on the tool, this may send relevant footage, audio, text, or images to that provider. The skill alone does not provide a fixed set of those services. Review the selected provider and its privacy policy before sending private material. Importing the skill is not authorization to publish your content.

Your host agent processes conversations and attachments under its own policy. For Claude, see [Anthropic’s Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy); other hosts have their own policies and settings.

## Publisher collection and optional email registration

The distributed configuration disables email registration and contains no collector endpoint, credentials, or recipient identity. It does not automatically send usage reports, email addresses, or project files to the skill publisher. Downloading or installing through GitHub or a marketplace is handled by that platform under its own policies.

A publisher of a modified distribution may enable the included registration helper. Before collecting an email address, that publisher must disclose the recipient, endpoint, purpose, retention period, and deletion contact and obtain informed consent. Registration can then send the email address and consent-related skill/version information to that disclosed endpoint. The registration helper is not enabled in this release. A local registration receipt must not contain the raw email address.

## Retention and deletion

Local project files remain where you save them until you delete them; TalkCraft does not run an automatic deletion schedule. Delete the project files, temporary media, and local registration receipts you no longer need. Your own backups or cloud-synced folders may retain copies according to their settings.

Content saved to Ancher can remain in your account beyond 30 days; retention, account deletion, backups, and privacy requests are governed by Ancher’s policy and controls. Other remote providers determine retention for the tools you select. Removing the skill does not delete content already saved in your project or sent to a service.

## Questions and requests

For skill-related questions, use the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/vincentwu821/ancher-talkcraft/issues). Do not post private footage, credentials, email addresses, or other personal information in a public issue; request a private contact channel if needed. For Ancher account or stored-content privacy requests, follow the contact instructions in Ancher’s Privacy Policy.

This skill is intended for a general adult creator audience, not specifically for users under 18. Material changes to this policy will be documented in the repository.
